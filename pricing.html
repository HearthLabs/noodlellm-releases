Common questions

Is there a limit on how much I can use it?
No. It runs on your own computer, so there is no charge per message and no daily cap. Use it as much as you like.
Does anything I type get sent to you?
No. Your questions, answers and files never leave your computer. The app only goes online to download models and check your subscription.
What happens after the 14 days?
The app pauses until you subscribe. Your models and conversations stay on your computer, and nothing is deleted.
Can I use it on more than one computer?
Yes, on up to 3 of your computers. To use a fourth, sign one of the others out from the app.
Will my $8 go up later?
For the first 100 members, you keep $8 a month, or $80 a year, for as long as you stay subscribed, even after the price for new members rises.
Can I cancel?
Yes, at any time, from the Account section of the app. Models you bought outright stay yours.
We are a firm. Do we each need a subscription?
No. The Practices plan covers up to 15 computers in one office for one price.
