What it does for you

The app speaks English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese, and most models answer in whichever language you write in.

It reads your own files

Point it at a folder of Word documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, slides or notes and ask about them. Before reading, it tells you how long it will take and which files it has to skip, and every answer names the file it came from.

It remembers you

Tell it once who you are and how you like answers written, and every conversation starts from there. It is a box you can read, edit and empty, and starting a message with "remember:" adds to it.

Long conversations do not fall apart

When a chat grows longer than the model can hold, it condenses the earlier messages into a summary instead of forgetting them, so names, figures and decisions from the start still count. You can read that summary at any time.

Tasks for jobs you repeat

Save a job you do often, like answering an email or turning notes into minutes, with its own instructions. Then paste in what you have and press Run. Four ready-made tasks are there to start from.

Teach it your style

Give it a folder of your own writing and it learns to sound like you, then use what it learned in any task. Teaching needs a Windows PC with an NVIDIA graphics card, and arrives in an update soon.

Use it from your phone

Pair your phone once by scanning a code, then carry on your conversations from the sofa or the next room, with your files included. It works through Wi-Fi and everything is encrypted between the two, so nothing gets out.

Why run it yourself

The cloud is smarter, but that was never the whole question. Use both. Keep a cloud AI such as ChatGPT for the rare problem that needs the very best, since your local AI may be weaker at hard reasoning questions than cloud-based models. Do the everyday, the private and the repetitive here: drafting a letter, summarising a report, finding the clause in a contract.

  1. 01

    They set the terms. You live with them.

    Cloud AI companies decide the price, the limits and which model you get, and change them when it suits them. On your computer nothing changes unless you change it.

  2. 02

    Try again as often as you like.

    Good results come from rewording, retrying and refining. When every attempt is metered, you hold back. On your own computer the tenth try costs the same as the first: nothing extra.

  3. 03

    Your files never leave the room.

    Nothing you type or open is uploaded, so there is nothing to leak and nobody else's privacy policy to trust. Once a model is installed it does not need the internet at all.

It writes like you, from your own files

Turn on your files and a skill you taught it, and it drafts the reply the way you would, naming the documents each part came from. The model, the skill and your files never leave your computer.
