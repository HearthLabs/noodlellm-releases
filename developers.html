Put your model in front of people who can run it

If you fine-tune models or build adapters, list them in the store, free. Your files stay on Hugging Face; the store shows each model to the people whose computer can run it and installs it in one click. Nobody pays per token and nobody rents a GPU.

Free to list

No fee and nothing to upload. The store links the exact file on your Hugging Face page.

Shown to people it will run on

Every listing is checked against each person's actual hardware, so they know whether it will run before they download it.

A page of your own

A creator page in the store with all your models, your name and a few words about you.

Selling comes later

Paid listings are on the way: you will set a price and keep 75% of every sale. Listing now gets your models reviewed and in the store first.

All self-service, start to finish

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign in with your email address. There is no application form and no call to book.

  2. 2

    Accept the Creator Agreement

    Plain terms: listing is free, and you can withdraw a listing at any time.

  3. 3

    Link your model

    Describe it, then paste the Hugging Face link for each version. The repository has to be public.

  4. 4

    We check it, then it goes live

    Every listing is reviewed before anyone can see it, and people are only shown models their computer can run.

  5. 5

    Keep it up to date

    Add a new version whenever you release one. The versions people already have never change under them.
