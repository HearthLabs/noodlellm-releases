Sell the models you already make

If you fine-tune models or build adapters, the store will be somewhere to sell them. This is not a hosting service: buyers run your model on their own machine, so nobody pays per token and nobody rents a GPU.

Keep 75% of what you sell

Higher than the app stores take, and affordable here because we host catalog files rather than GPUs. The infrastructure cost of your work is close to nothing.

Charge for work you give away today

A fine-tune uploaded to a model hub earns nothing, however good it is.

We handle payment and licensing

You upload the file and set a price. Purchases, licence keys and updates are ours to run, and licences verify on the buyer's machine without phoning home.

Only shown to people it will run on

Every listing is checked against the buyer's actual hardware first. Nobody pays for a model their computer cannot load, which is the usual source of refunds and one-star reviews.

All self-service, start to finish

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign in with your email address. There is no application form and no call to book.

  2. 2

    Set up how you are paid

    Connect Stripe and your share reaches your bank at every sale. Where Stripe cannot pay out, choose PayPal, Payoneer or Wise and be paid monthly.

  3. 3

    Accept the Creator Agreement

    Plain terms: you keep 75% of every sale and can withdraw a listing at any time.

  4. 4

    Upload and set a price

    Describe your model, upload the file and choose a price, or make it free.

  5. 5

    We check it, then it goes live

    Every listing is reviewed before buyers can see it, and buyers are only shown models their computer can run.

  6. 6

    Watch your sales

    See every sale, what you have earned and what is still to be paid out, whenever you like.

The creator side is not open yet. This is what it will be when it is, and the terms above are the ones we intend to launch with.
