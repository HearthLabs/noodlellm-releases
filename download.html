What it costs

Everything is free for the first 14 days: the whole app, every open model in the catalog, no card required to start. After that it is a subscription, paid monthly or yearly.

Some specialist models in the store are sold separately by the people who made them. Those are a one-off purchase rather than a rental: buy one and it stays yours.

Windows will warn you. Here is why.

When you run the installer, Windows shows a blue screen saying “Windows protected your PC”. There is no obvious way past it: you have to click More info, then Run anyway.

This is not a virus warning. Windows shows it for any installer that has not yet been signed with a certificate it recognises, which costs several hundred pounds a year and takes weeks to issue. We are getting one. Until then, we would rather tell you about the warning than have you meet it unprepared.

What it needs

A PC running Windows 10 or 11
64-bit. Nothing else to install: no Python, no command line, no configuration.
Any graphics card, or none
NVIDIA, AMD and Intel all work. Without one, models run on the processor instead: slower, but it works.
Room for the models
The app is 19 MB. Models are separate downloads, usually 2–10 GB each, and you choose which.
An internet connection, now and then
To download models and to keep your subscription current. Day to day it runs with the network switched off; your questions never need it.

Not sure what your computer can handle? See the models grouped by graphics card — or just install it, and it will tell you.
