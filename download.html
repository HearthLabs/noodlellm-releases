What it costs

Everything is free for the first 14 days: the whole app, every open model in the catalog, no card required to start. After that it is a subscription, paid monthly or yearly.

Windows will warn you the first time.

When you run the installer, Windows shows a blue screen saying “Windows protected your PC”. There is no obvious way past it: you have to click More info, then Run anyway.

This is not a virus warning. Windows shows it for new apps it does not recognise yet, and only the first time.

Your Mac will ask too.

The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious software. This is not a virus warning either. Click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. It only asks once.

What it needs

Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac with Apple Silicon
64-bit Windows, or an M1 or newer Mac on macOS 13.3 or later. Nothing else to install: no Python, no command line, no configuration.
Any graphics card, or none
NVIDIA, AMD and Intel all work, and on a Mac its built-in graphics do. Without one, models run on the processor instead: slower, but it works.
Room for the models
The app is 19 MB on Windows and 31 MB on a Mac. Models are separate downloads, usually 2–10 GB each, and you choose which.
An internet connection, now and then
To download models and to keep your subscription current. Day to day it runs with the network switched off; your questions never need it.

Not sure what your computer can handle? Install it, and it will tell you.
