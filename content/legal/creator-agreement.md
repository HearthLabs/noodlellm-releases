# Creator Agreement

**Last updated:** 12 October 2026 · Version 2026-10

This agreement is between you, the person or company listing a model ("**you**", the "**Creator**"), and **Noodle Technology, LLC** ("**Noodle LLM**", "**we**", "**us**"). It applies when you use the creator portal at noodlellm.com/portal to list a model in the Noodle LLM store. Our [Terms and Conditions](/terms) and [Privacy Statement](/privacy) also apply. You accept it by ticking the box in the portal.

## 1. What you list

1.1 A "**Listing**" is a model you upload or link, the files that make it up, and the name, description and other details you write about it.

1.2 You promise that for every Listing:

- you created the model, or its licence lets you share it and, if you charge for it, sell it;
- the licence you pick in the portal is the licence the model is really under, and you include any notice that licence requires;
- the description is accurate, including what the model is good and bad at, and the memory figures are honest;
- the files are a working GGUF model and nothing else: no malware, no hidden code, nothing that tries to reach the network;
- the model was not trained to produce child sexual abuse material, to help with weapons capable of mass casualties, or to get around the law, and it does not contain personal data you had no right to use.

1.3 You keep all your rights in your model. You give us a non-exclusive, worldwide licence to store, review, copy, distribute and sell it through Noodle LLM's website and app, to show its name and description, and to use them to promote the store, for as long as the Listing is live and afterwards for the copies covered by section 5.3.

1.4 **Linked files.** A free Listing links its files on Hugging Face instead of uploading them to us. We record the exact version of each file you link, and the app downloads it from Hugging Face. Keep those files public and unchanged for as long as the Listing is live. If one disappears, becomes private or gated, or no longer matches what we recorded, we may take the Listing down until you add a working version.

## 2. Review

2.1 We review every Listing before it appears in the store. We may download and run your files, and we may ask you to change a Listing or decline it, with a reason.

2.2 We may take a Listing down at any time if we reasonably believe it breaks this agreement, infringes someone's rights, is harmful, or if we receive a credible legal complaint about it. We will tell you why, unless the law stops us.

2.3 Approval is not an endorsement, and it does not shift your responsibility for the model to us.

## 3. Price and our share

3.0 **Paid Listings are not open yet.** Until we tell you they are, every Listing is free, and sections 3.1 to 4.4 do not apply. We will tell you by email before paid Listings open.

3.1 You choose whether a Listing is free or paid. A paid Listing has two one-off prices in US dollars: a personal price, between US$1 and US$1,000, for one buyer on up to 3 computers; and a practice price, at least the personal price and up to US$10,000, for a firm or practice on up to 15 computers. The portal suggests a practice price of five times the personal price.

3.2 We sell paid Listings to buyers on your behalf. For each sale, you receive **75%** of the price the buyer paid, and Noodle LLM keeps **25%**. Our share covers card processing fees, hosting and downloads, and running the store. Sales tax or VAT that we collect is not part of the price for this purpose.

3.3 If a buyer is refunded, or a payment is disputed or reversed, your share of that sale is cancelled. If you were already paid it, we may take it back from your Stripe account or deduct it from your next payout.

## 4. Getting paid

4.1 **Stripe.** If you choose Stripe, you open a Stripe Express account through the portal and agree to [Stripe's Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/connect-account/legal). Your share of each sale is transferred to your Stripe account when the sale happens, and Stripe pays it out to your bank on its own schedule.

4.2 **PayPal, Payoneer or Wise.** If Stripe cannot pay out in your country, you may choose one of these. We pay everything owed to you once a month, in the first 7 days of the month, to the account email you give us, once your balance is at least US$25. Any fees those services charge to receive the money are yours.

4.3 **Tax.** You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn. Before we pay you, we need the right US tax form from you: a W-9 if you are a US person or company, or a W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E if you are not. Stripe collects this for Stripe payouts; for the others, we ask for it by email. We may withhold payment, or withhold tax where the law requires it, until we have it.

4.4 Keep your payout details up to date. We are not responsible for a payment sent to the details you gave us.

## 5. Changing or removing a Listing

5.1 To change a live Listing, withdraw it in the portal, edit it, and submit it again for review. Withdrawing takes it out of the store straight away.

5.2 You must never change a file that people have already downloaded: add a new version instead. A file's fingerprint (its SHA-256) is how the app checks that a buyer got exactly what they paid for.

5.3 Buyers keep the right to use, and to download again, any model they bought, even if you withdraw it or this agreement ends. We will keep those files available to them for that purpose.

## 6. Responsibility

6.1 You will compensate Noodle LLM for claims, losses and reasonable legal costs that arise because a Listing broke your promises in section 1.2.

6.2 The store is provided as it is. Except where the law does not allow it, our total liability to you under this agreement is limited to the amount we owed you in the 12 months before the claim.

## 7. Ending this agreement

7.1 You can stop at any time by withdrawing your Listings and emailing [info@noodletechnology.com](mailto:info@noodletechnology.com). We pay what is owed to you in the next payout.

7.2 We can end this agreement on 30 days' notice, or immediately if you seriously break it.

## 8. Changes

We may update this agreement. We will email you at least 30 days before a change takes effect, and the portal will ask you to accept the new version before you submit another Listing. If you do not agree, you can withdraw your Listings.
