Licences and subscriptions

How a Noodle LLM licence works: which plan covers what, how many computers each one is good for, and what happens when you add a computer, go offline or cancel. This page explains the Terms in plain words. If the two ever disagree, the Terms are what count.

Start with the free trial

Every new install gets 14 days free. You do not need an account or a payment card. When the trial ends, the app pauses until you subscribe. Your models and conversations stay on your computer, nothing is deleted, and nothing is charged automatically.

The three plans

  • Founding member: $8 a month or $80 a year. One person, on up to 3 computers. Only for the first 100 subscribers.
  • Standard: $29 a month or $300 a year. One person, on up to 3 computers.
  • Practice: $200 a month or $2,000 a year. Everyone working in one firm, practice or office, on up to 15 computers.

All three include everything: every model in the catalogue, no per-message charges and no daily caps. They differ only in who may use them and on how many computers.

Three computers or fifteen: which one fits

A personal licence (Founding member or Standard) covers one person on up to 3 computers. That means your own computers, for example a desktop at home, a laptop and a work PC. It is not meant to be shared with family or colleagues, even if you have a computer to spare.

A Practice licence covers the people in one firm, practice or office on up to 15 computers. You pay one price for the office. There are no per-person seats, so a receptionist, two partners and an assistant can all use it. Only the number of computers counts.

How to choose:

  • Just you: Standard, or Founding member while places last.
  • A small office with 2 or 3 people: buying one personal plan each usually costs less ($29 a month per person) until you get to around 7 people. Each person needs their own plan, because a personal licence is for one person.
  • More people than that, or computers that several people share: Practice. It covers up to 15 computers for a single price, and you do not have to track who uses which one.
  • More than 15 computers: email info@noodlellm.com.

How a licence is applied to a computer

  1. You subscribe from the app. Settings → Account opens Stripe's checkout in your browser. When you have paid, the app picks up your licence by itself. You never need to copy a key.
  2. The computer is added to your licence. The app makes a random ID for this install when it first runs, and that ID is what counts as "a computer". It is not a hardware fingerprint, and nothing about your machine is sent beyond its name, so you can recognise it in the list.
  3. To add another computer, install the app, open Settings → Account and choose Already subscribed? Use the email you paid with. We email a 6-digit code to the address you used at checkout, and entering it moves the licence onto that computer.
  4. When you reach the limit (a 4th computer on a personal plan, a 16th on Practice), the app shows the computers already using the licence, each with a Sign out button. Sign one out and the new computer takes its place in the same step.
  5. Changing or retiring a computer. Choose Settings → Account → Sign out of this computer to free its place straight away. If you don't, a computer the app has not seen online for 60 days stops counting by itself.

Working offline

Your licence is a digitally signed key stored on your computer. The app checks it without going online, so Noodle LLM keeps working on a plane, in a cabin, or anywhere with the internet switched off.

Each key lasts about 35 days. Whenever the computer is online, the app quietly renews it, so most people never notice. A computer that stays offline for more than about 35 days pauses until it can go online once to renew.

The licence check never sends your conversations or documents anywhere. Those stay on your computer whatever plan you are on. The Privacy Statement lists exactly what the licence check sends.

How the subscription runs

  • Monthly or yearly. A yearly plan costs about two months less than paying monthly.
  • It renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel. Stripe handles the payment, and we never see your card number.
  • The founding price is locked in. The first 100 subscribers keep $8 a month for as long as their subscription stays active, even if prices rise for everyone else. If you cancel and come back later, you pay the price at that time.
  • Price changes for other plans apply from your next renewal, and we tell you at least 30 days ahead.
  • Cancel any time from Settings → Account (it opens Stripe's billing page) or by emailing us. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After that the app pauses as it does when a trial ends, and nothing on your computer is deleted.
  • Refunds and chargebacks. If a payment is refunded or disputed with your bank, the licence stops working on every computer the next time each one goes online.

Models from creators

Every model in the store is free with any plan today. Creators will be able to sell their own models later. When they can, a creator model will be a one-off purchase you keep, not a subscription, and it will use the same two sizes: a personal price for one person on up to 3 computers, and a practice price for a firm on up to 15. Each computer that downloads the model counts towards that limit, and a computer unseen for 60 days frees its place, just as with your subscription.
