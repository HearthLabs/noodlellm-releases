The strongest thinker here: analysis, maths and code on a 24 GB card

Alibaba's current 27B, and the best general-purpose answer for a 24GB card. It is better than anything smaller here at reasoning that takes several steps, and the gap widens on questions where the obvious answer is wrong.

Choose it if you have a 24GB card and want one model for everything. Consider instead Gemma 4 31B if most of your work is writing rather than analysis — they are close, and Gemma has the better prose.
